== Terminology maintenance for geographic information

Reese Plews is a long-standing leader in the management and maintenance of
standardized terminology, best known as Convenor of the Terminology Maintenance
Group (TMG) of ISO/TC 211, Geographic information/Geomatics.

== Contributions to standards

* Convenor of the ISO/TC 211 Terminology Maintenance Group (TMG), overseeing the
  committee's terminology work including its Multi-Lingual Glossary of Terms, and
  providing editorial guidance to the committee's project leaders and editors
* Project leader of SANS 1883, the South African address standard that became a key
  input to the ISO 19160 (Addressing) series
* Project leadership of FERIN, the Framework for Extensible Registration of
  Information, standardized in ISO 19135:2026

== At Enosema

* President of the Enosema Foundation
* Leads the Foundation's standardization agenda for shared concepts and registers
