== Standards identification and terminology

Joanna Goodwin spent her career where standards publishing meets terminology
management, serving international standardization at the International Organization
for Standardization (ISO) and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC),
from which she has since retired.

== Contributions to standards

* Author of RFC 5141, _A Uniform Resource Name (URN) Namespace for the International
  Organization for Standardization (ISO)_ (IETF, 2008), with Holger Apel — the
  specification behind the `urn:iso:std:` identifiers, which give ISO standards,
  their editions, supplements, languages, and even individual clauses, tables, and
  terms persistent, location-independent identification on the Internet
* Named in RFC 5141 as the declared registrant of the ISO URN namespace, on behalf
  of the ISO Central Secretariat
* Former Terminology Coordinator at the IEC, coordinating terminology across the
  IEC's technical committees
* Former secretary of IEC/TC 1, Terminology — the committee that maintains the
  International Electrotechnical Vocabulary (IEV)

== At Enosema

* Founder of the Enosema Foundation
* Terminology strategy and liaison with the ISO and IEC communities
