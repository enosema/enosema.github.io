== Standards production and terminology

Joanna Goodwin spent a 25-year career in international standardization — 15
years at the ISO Central Secretariat, then a decade at the International
Electrotechnical Commission (IEC), from which she has since retired.

== Previous roles

* *Terminology Coordinator, IEC (2011–2021)* — oversaw the maintenance,
  evolution, and harmonization of the IEC's terminology collections and
  databases, and the mechanisms and processes for terminology development,
  harmonization, and capture
* *Manager, Production Services, ISO Central Secretariat (2004–2011)* — managed
  production services for International Standards (30 staff); drafted and
  maintained the editorial rules for International Standards, subsequently
  adopted by several regional and national standards bodies; project leader for
  production improvement and internal auditor
* *Assistant Director, Production Management, ISO Central Secretariat
  (1996–2004)* — managed the production line for International Standards across
  editing, technical draughting, composition, proofreading, and metadata;
  reengineered the production process to halve production time and introduced
  SGML electronic publishing across the development–publication–revision loop

== Contributions to standards

* Author of RFC 5141, _A Uniform Resource Name (URN) Namespace for the
  International Organization for Standardization (ISO)_ (IETF, 2008), with
  Holger Apel — written during her tenure at the ISO Central Secretariat — the
  specification behind the `urn:iso:std:` identifiers, which give ISO standards,
  their editions, supplements, languages, and even individual clauses, tables,
  and terms persistent, location-independent identification on the Internet
* Declared registrant of the ISO URN namespace, named in RFC 5141
* Secretary of IEC/TC 1, Terminology — the committee that maintains the
  International Electrotechnical Vocabulary (IEV)
* Organized the IEC workshop on the revised International System of Units (SI)
  with IEC/TC 1 chair Luca Mari at the 2019 IEC General Meeting in Shanghai
