== Open standards, date and time, and semantic technology

Ronald Tse is the founder and CEO of Ribose and a long-time contributor to
international standardization, working on open standards and the machine-readable
modeling of concepts.

== Contributions to standards

* Convenor of ISO/TC 154/WG 5, Date and time — the working group responsible for
  the ISO 8601 series on date and time representations
* Project leader of ISO/NP 23404
* Project leader of FERIN, the Framework for Extensible Registration of Information
* Vice President and Director of External Relations of CalConnect, The Calendaring
  and Scheduling Consortium
* Has served on CSA's International Standardization Council and as an expert at
  ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 27

== At Enosema

* Founder of the Enosema Foundation
